💡 Study Tips for Success

  • Practice Regularly: Spend 15-20 minutes daily on these topics alongside your school work
  • Use the Cheat Sheets: Print them out and keep them handy during practice
  • Ask Questions: If something doesn't make sense, ask for clarification immediately
  • Connect Concepts: Try to see how binary links to Boolean logic, and how both connect to programming
  • Practice by Hand: Don't rely on calculators - practice conversions and logic by hand first

💪 Remember

"The best way to learn computer science is to think like a computer scientist - break problems into smaller parts and solve them step by step!"
